Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Information and files

4K AI-Studio does not currently provide user accounts. Projects are saved locally in your browser unless you export them. Video files selected for local editing are processed in your browser and are not uploaded by the editor.

AI features

If you use an AI assistant or generation feature, the text, image or prompt needed for that feature is sent to the configured provider (such as OpenAI or fal.ai) through this app's server. Provider terms and privacy policies apply. Do not submit confidential or sensitive material.

Payments

The app does not collect payments or sell subscriptions at this time. If paid AI generation is enabled, the configured AI provider may charge the account owner for successful outputs. Review provider pricing before use.

Data retention and contact

Local projects can be removed by clearing this site's browser data. Server logs may contain technical request information for reliability and security. For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the repository support page.

Children

This creator tool is not designed specifically for children. Users should follow the age requirements and terms of the AI providers they choose.
